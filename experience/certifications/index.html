      Teaching & Professional Certifications

      Professional certifications in teaching English as a second language, technical writing, and language pedagogy.

      2017
      TESL Certification

      University of North Texas

      Teaching English as a Second Language (TESL) certification earned as part of the Master's program in Linguistics. This certification validates expertise in ESL instructional methods, curriculum design, assessment, and cross-cultural communication.

      2014
      CELTA (Certificate in Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages)

      Cambridge Assessment English • University of Texas at Austin

      Completed the internationally recognized CELTA certification, the gold standard for English language teaching. This intensive program covers classroom management, lesson planning, language analysis, and practical teaching skills with real ESL students.

      2014
      TEFL Certification (Teaching English as a Foreign Language)

      University of Texas at Austin

      Earned TEFL certification providing comprehensive training in teaching English to non-native speakers. Covers methodology, materials development, and practical teaching techniques for diverse learning environments.

      2013
      Technical Writing Certificate

      University of North Texas

      Professional certificate in Technical Writing from UNT's Department of Technical Communication. Training includes document design, user-centered content development, editing for clarity, and creating technical documentation for diverse audiences.
