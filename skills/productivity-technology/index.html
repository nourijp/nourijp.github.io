      Technology That Works For Me

      I don't accept default settings. Every tool I use is customized to fit my workflow, not the other way around. From keyboard shortcuts to automation scripts, I build personalized systems that eliminate friction and amplify productivity across all my devices.

      Raycast & Launcher Customization

      • Custom scripts for opening specific sites in specific browser profiles
      • Quick actions for any app function without memorizing shortcuts
      • Contextual commands that adapt to current application
      • Snippets and text expansion for common responses
      • Window management with keyboard-driven precision

      iOS Shortcuts & Automation

      • Complex multi-step workflows triggered by a single tap or keyword
      • Location-based and time-based automations
      • Integration between apps that don't natively connect
      • Quick capture systems for notes, tasks, and media
      • Custom Share Sheet actions for rapid information processing

      macOS Deep Customization

      • Hidden preferences unlocked via Terminal commands

      • Dotfiles versioned and portable across machines
      • Server administration and automation scripts

      Philosophy

      Every click you eliminate is a micro-win. Every workflow you automate gives you time back for the work that actually matters. I obsess over these small optimizations because they compound — and because a tool that works exactly how you think should feel like magic.
